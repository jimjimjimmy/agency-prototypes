Flow Lab Week 1: Sign‑in

Mobile only. A returning customer on the couch who just wants to check an order. Four ways to sign in, each testing one hypothesis. Every flow is clickable from sign-in to order status, with its failure and reset states.

  • Hypothesis 1

    Passkey-first

    A passkey as the main action, password as the fallback.
  • Hypothesis 2

    Guest order status

    No password: an emailed sign-in link goes straight to the order.
  • Hypothesis 3

    Social over OTP

    Google or Apple first, an emailed code as the fallback.
  • Hypothesis 4

    OTP auto-read

    A texted code that fills itself in, instead of a password.

Prototypes with test copy. No real sign-in happens and nothing you type is sent anywhere. Built with Material 3 and the staple. identity.
